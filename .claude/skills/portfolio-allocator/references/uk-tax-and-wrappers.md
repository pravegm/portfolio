# UK Tax and Wrappers

Used in Phases 0, 5, 6 and 9. Figures below were checked in October 2026 for the 2026/27 tax year (6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027). **Re-verify thresholds and platform fees each tax year and whenever a Budget changes them**, and update this file. This is analysis, not tax advice; for anything material, suggest an accountant.

## Wrappers

**Stocks & Shares ISA**
- £20,000 allowance per person per tax year across all ISAs. Resets 6 April; unused allowance is lost.
- Since 6 April 2024 a person can pay into more than one stocks & shares ISA in the same tax year (provider permitting), as long as the total stays within £20,000.
- From 6 April 2027 the cash ISA limit falls to £12,000 for under-65s; the overall £20,000 and the stocks & shares limit are unchanged.
- Gains, dividends and interest are tax-free and don't need reporting. Losses inside an ISA can't be used against gains elsewhere.
- Eligible: shares listed on a recognised stock exchange (e.g. LSE main market, AIM, NYSE, Nasdaq), UCITS funds and ETFs, gilts and bonds. Not eligible: OTC-only shares, crypto, CFDs. Each broker also has its own ISA list; check the instrument is available **inside that broker's ISA**, not just on the platform.
- Lifetime ISA (18-39 to open): £4,000 a year inside the £20,000, 25% bonus, for a first home (price cap applies) or age 60+; 25% withdrawal charge otherwise.

**General investment account (GIA)** (the eToro account, unless it is an eToro ISA)
- Capital gains tax on disposals above the annual exempt amount; dividend tax on dividends above the allowance.
- Losses offset gains in the same year and can be carried forward if reported to HMRC within 4 years of the end of the tax year they arose in.
- Crypto held on eToro is taxed under CGT, with its own pooling rules.
- ETFs held in a GIA should have UK "reporting fund" status; gains on non-reporting offshore funds are taxed as income.

## Household planning (married couple)

- **Transfers between spouses are tax-neutral** (no gain, no loss): the receiver takes over the original cost. This lets the couple use **both** CGT exempt amounts (£6,000 a year together) and put gains with whoever pays the lower rate. The transfer must be a genuine gift.
- Two ISA allowances: £40,000 a year of tax-free capacity together; at £9,600 a year each in ISAs there is plenty of room.
- Two dividend allowances, two basic-rate bands for CGT.
- Avoid both partners building the same concentrated bets without meaning to (the household overlap check).

## Key rules for sells and harvesting

- **30-day rule (bed and breakfasting):** if you sell shares and buy the same shares back in the same account type within 30 days, the sale is matched with the repurchase for CGT, so the gain or loss isn't crystallised as intended.
- **Bed-and-ISA:** sell in the GIA and buy the same holding inside an ISA. Commonly used to move holdings into the tax shelter and to crystallise gains up to the exempt amount (or losses). Uses ISA allowance. Generally accepted practice; confirm the treatment with an accountant before relying on it for losses.
- **Bed-and-spouse:** one spouse sells and the other buys. Also commonly used.
- Report disposals on a self-assessment return when gains exceed the exempt amount or total disposal proceeds exceed £50,000 in the year. Deadline for online returns: 31 January after the tax year.
- Keep records: eToro annual account statements give cost and proceeds. The history file keeps a realized-gains log for the GIA so the CGT allowance can be tracked during the year.

## Asset location

Where each holding should live:
- **Highest expected growth and anything likely to be sold at a large gain belongs in an ISA.** A venture shot that goes 10x in a GIA could hand 18-24% of the gain above the allowance to HMRC.
- **GIA is for what can't go in an ISA** (crypto, OTC names) and for positions where usable losses are likely and valuable.
- eToro offers a Stocks & Shares ISA (DIY, via Moneyfarm). It removed commission and custody fees in July 2026; FX charges still apply on non-GBP assets. Because multiple stocks & shares ISAs are allowed per year, new venture shots could be bought inside an eToro ISA while staying on eToro, if the name is ISA-eligible there. Raise this as an option in diagnostics (it changes the wrapper, not the strategy); the user decides.
- Moving existing GIA holdings into an ISA is a bed-and-ISA: crystallises gains (use the exempt amount, spread across tax years and spouses) and uses allowance.

## Withholding tax

- US dividends: 15% US withholding with a W-8BEN on file (30% without). Check the W-8BEN is in place on each platform. Inside an ISA the 15% can't be reclaimed.
- Irish-domiciled UCITS ETFs holding US stocks suffer 15% at fund level and nothing further for a UK investor; US-domiciled ETFs are generally not available to UK retail investors (PRIIPs).
- Mostly a minor drag for growth-oriented holdings; it matters for income-focused picks.

## Platform costs (verify each tax year)

| Platform | What to check |
|---|---|
| eToro (GIA) | Per-trade commission on stocks, FX conversion on GBP deposits into the USD account, spreads on crypto, withdrawal fee |
| eToro ISA | No commission or custody fee since July 2026; FX charge on non-GBP assets; ISA instrument list |
| Trading 212 ISA | No commission; 0.15% FX fee on non-GBP trades and dividends; card deposit fees beyond a threshold (bank transfer free) |
| Revolut ISA | Free orders per month depend on the subscription plan, with commission beyond that; FX fee varies by plan (reported up to ~1%). **With 8 ISA orders a month, check how many are free on the user's plan.** |

Per-month cost check: number of orders x commission + FX % x non-GBP amount + fund charges. On £100 orders, a £1 fee is 1%; prefer GBP-traded lines and batching where the platform charges per order.

## Tax-year checklist (run in March and April)

1. ISA allowance used this year (each partner); top up before 5 April if cash is available and the policy wants it.
2. GIA realized gains so far vs the £3,000 exempt amount (each partner). If unused, consider crystallising gains up to it via bed-and-ISA.
3. Losses: crystallise any that are usable this year or worth carrying forward; make sure past losses were reported.
4. Spouse transfers to use both exempt amounts or the lower rate.
5. Dividend allowance usage in the GIA.
6. Download annual statements (eToro account statement, ISA statements) for records.
7. Re-verify the figures in this file for the new tax year; update it.
8. Re-confirm the investment policy and financial foundations (annual review).
