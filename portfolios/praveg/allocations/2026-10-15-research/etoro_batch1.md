## 14. Consolidation notes (eToro taxable to Revolut S&S ISA)

**Mechanics.** An ISA cannot take shares in specie from a GIA. A MOVE means selling in eToro, withdrawing, subscribing cash to the ISA (within the £20,000 annual allowance), then rebuying. Every MOVE therefore **crystallises a CGT gain or loss** in GBP terms (FX matters).
