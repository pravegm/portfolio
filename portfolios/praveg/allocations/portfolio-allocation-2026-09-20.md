## 1. Global market scan

**The regime changed this month. This is the single most important fact in the report.**

**Rates:** The Fed **hiked 25bp on 16 September — its first hike in three years** — and signalled more to come. Fed funds move to 3.75-4.00%. The 10-year Treasury sits near **4.97-5.00%**, up from 4.71% in August. August payrolls came in at 162,000 against 53,000 expected; August PPI ran **+5.4% year-on-year**, accelerating from 4.8%, driven by energy.

**Energy — the engine of everything else:** Brent has gone from $87-90 in August to **above $105**. US-Iran diplomacy collapsed entirely, with direct attacks on tankers over 5-6 September. **Strait of Hormuz transits have fallen from roughly 125 ships a day to 7-14** — an 80%+ collapse, which the IEA calls **"the largest supply disruption in the history of the global oil market."** Add the Bab al-Mandab closure and the East-West pipeline shutdown on 11 September, and roughly **39% of global trade** is disrupted. US diesel passed **$6 a gallon for the first time ever** on refining capacity shortages. For context on how unexpected this is: Goldman's 2026 outlook modelled Brent averaging $56.

**Equities:** S&P 500 around 7,620-7,680, off its 7,800 August record. On 14 September a semiconductor gauge fell **5.9% in a single day** after the CEOs of Anthropic, OpenAI and xAI called for slowing frontier AI development on safety grounds. *(Disclosure: Anthropic built me. I've tried to treat this as I would any other market-moving story, but you should know the connection.)* Barclays nonetheless raised its S&P target to 7,950 on 9 September.

**Crypto:** **Bitcoin reclaimed $80,000, up ~29% from August's $62.6k**, with SOL +43%, MARA +45%, COIN +28%, BNB +25%. The drivers were SEC and CFTC rulemaking advancing (the SEC proposed letting startups sell up to $75M of tokens without registration) plus a **$2.7B short squeeze** — and notably this happened *despite* the **Clarity Act failing its 15 September cloture vote**. BTC remains ~40% below its October 2025 peak of $126,000, and analysts are clear the bear market only ends if it *holds* above $80k rather than tagging it.

**Shipping:** the year's hottest trade. A basket of 35 shipping stocks is **+68% YTD, five times the S&P**, with **crude tankers +120%**.

**Regions:** consensus 2026 EPS growth is **31% US, 17% Europe, 18% Japan, and over 70% in EM** (Goldman Sachs AM). Morgan Stanley sees global GDP at 3.2% for 2026, flagging Europe as most exposed to energy disruption. Gold is becalmed near **$4,380** — "technical no-man's land," competing with ~5% yields — though US public debt crossing **$40 trillion** in August supports it structurally.

**What this means for you:** rising discount rates compress long-duration assets. Your portfolio is roughly 90% long-duration growth with **no fixed income, no consumer staples, and only one energy ETF**. That is the vulnerability September exposed.
