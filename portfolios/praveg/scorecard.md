## Summary (as of 2026-10-15)
- **Venture:** 5 of 16 beat the Russell 2000 (31%); median excess -12.5 points. Real-revenue names (TEM, S, PHR, PGY) are the winners; pre-profit story names (NNE, IONQ, WULF, MP) lost 23-49 points.
- **ISA:** 10 of 24 beat the S&P 500 (42%); median excess -2.7 points. Quality large-caps median about +3.5 (META, V, XOM, BRK, DE, ISRG ahead; VRT, AMZN, JPM behind). ETFs median about -5 (regional/core-diversifiers EM, Europe, India, small cap and several sector ETFs trailed; LOCK, HEAL, IESU, ICSU ahead).
- **Process changes from this:** (1) venture picks need real revenue, strong gross margins and runway; no pre-revenue stories; (2) ISA satellite money favours quality large-caps over regional and narrow sector ETFs; (3) the S&P 500 beat most diversifiers this year, a point in favour of US-weighted core money within the 25% core cap.
